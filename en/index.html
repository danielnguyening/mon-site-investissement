My 5 rules

  1. 1
    Buy stocks in 5 instalments

    20M a month rather than 100M at once, to smooth out the purchase price.

  2. 2
    ETFs and funds first

    Diversified, low fees. No individual stocks at the start.

  3. 3
    Gold, for the long term only

    The buy / sell spread makes it unsuited to speculation.

  4. 4
    Rebalance once a year

    Return to the target shares: sell what has risen too much, top up the rest.

  5. 5
    Avoid the traps

    Peer-to-peer loans with promised returns, leveraged crypto, margin trading.
